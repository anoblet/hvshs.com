## Scheduling Notes

- **Best time to post:** Weekday mornings (7:30–9:00 AM) or early evenings
  (5:30–7:30 PM) when local homeowners are most active on Facebook.
- **Hashtags:** 10 tags keeps the post discoverable without looking spammy.
- **Image:** Use `facebook-home.png` as the attachment. Facebook recommends a
  1.91:1 (1200×630) crop for feed links — the 1280×800 capture works as-is, or
  crop the hero area for a tighter feed preview.
- **CTA options:** The post links to the free consultation calendar; consider
  pinning a comment with the direct booking link for easy tap-through on mobile.
