# Lutron vs. Control4 for Westchester Homes

Walk into almost any high-end Westchester home and you'll find either Lutron or Control4 (or both) behind the wall. Both are excellent, established brands — but they solve different problems, and the right choice depends on your home and goals.

### Lutron: lighting-first, retrofit-friendly

Lutron (Caséta Wireless, RA3, and HomeWorks) is the gold standard for **lighting control**. It's also one of the most retrofit-friendly systems around:

- Works with your home's existing wiring in most cases.
- Rock-solid reliability — lighting systems that just work for decades.
- Scales from a single switch to a whole estate.
- Integrates with Alexa, Google, Apple Home, and most automation platforms.

If your main goal is beautiful, reliable lighting and shade control without rewiring, Lutron is usually the right answer — especially in older Rivertown homes where running new wires is expensive.

### Control4: whole-home automation platform

Control4 is a **full automation platform**: lighting, climate, audio/video, security, and more under one roof. It's a common choice for larger new builds and whole-home installs because:

- One interface (and one dealer relationship) for everything.
- Powerful scenes and programming beyond lighting.
- Designed for homes with multiple systems to integrate.

The trade-off is that Control4 is dealer-installed, costs more up front, and is a proprietary ecosystem — you're generally tied to a certified integrator for changes.

### Which should you choose?

A quick rule of thumb:

- **Retrofitting an existing Westchester home, especially prewar construction** → Lutron lighting control plus smart devices that integrate with it.
- **New construction or a gut renovation with full AV and security scope** → Control4 (or another full platform) may justify its cost.
- **Not sure yet** → that's what a [smart home audit](/pricing) is for.
